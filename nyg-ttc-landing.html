Why this course exists

A certificate is just the beginning.

“Our society doesn't need more yoga certificates. It needs yoga teachers who can bring real benefit to society.”
— Nirvana Yoga Global

Every year, thousands complete a teacher training in India. The course ends, the certificate arrives — and most students are left wondering what to do next.

They know the asanas. They've studied the philosophy. But standing in front of a real class, guiding real people with real problems — that confidence was never built.

Our six-month TTC is designed around that gap: you learn yoga, and you learn how to teach it — therapeutically, practically, and with the steadiness that makes students trust you.

The path of a successful teacher

Three things every great yoga teacher must build.

1

Self-transformation first

You must be healthy, peaceful, and confident before you guide others. When your own practice is steady, students naturally place their trust in you.

2

Solutions, not just asanas

A teacher society needs can address sleeplessness, lifestyle diseases, and psychosomatic conditions — not just demonstrate postures.

3

A good teacher is a good learner

Certification is a milestone, not a destination. Lifetime mentorship, self-practice, and continued teaching experience make a career.

This is the Nirvana Yoga approach: we don't just teach you yoga — we train the teacher in you, in the therapeutic tradition of the Nirvana Yoga System.

Inside the six-month course

What you'll learn — and what you'll leave with.

✓

Teaching, taught properly

Asanas, anatomy, and philosophy — plus the art of actually leading a class: sequencing, instruction, presence, and confidence building.

✓

Therapeutic yoga training

Learn to teach people with different needs — lifestyle issues, psychosomatic conditions, sleeplessness — addressing the root, not just the symptom.

✓

Practical learning throughout

Supervised teaching practice across the six months, so your first real class isn't your first time teaching.

✓

YCB exam preparation

Structured preparation for the Yoga Certification Board examination, guided by AYUSH-empanelled faculty.

✓

One year of free mentorship

The course ends; the guidance doesn't. A full year of mentorship, career guidance, and training support at no extra cost.

✓

Niche specialisation guidance

Find your direction — pregnancy yoga, yoga for the elderly, corporate wellness — and build a career around it.
