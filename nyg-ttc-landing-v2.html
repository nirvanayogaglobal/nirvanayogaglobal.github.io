The Programme

A system that integrates everything.

Built on the teachings of Bodhisatvan Madhavacharyan, Founder of the Nirvana Yoga System — this is not a fixed routine taught to a room. It is a living integration of four pillars, delivered across six months.

01

Self-transformation, first

You must be healthy, peaceful, and confident before you guide anyone else. Deep experiential practice — you go through your own transformation before leading others through theirs.

02

Therapeutic yoga — solutions, not just asanas

Understanding the human system — body, mind, energy — and how to work with each student's unique reality: sleeplessness, lifestyle diseases, psychosomatic conditions. Solutions at the root, not routines at the surface.

03

Teaching & emotional guidance

How to hold space, guide students through real challenges, and lead a class with genuine presence and practical, hands-on teaching experience throughout the six months.

04

A lifelong learner, always

A good teacher is always a good learner. Certification is a beginning — continued masterclasses and mentorship carry you well beyond graduation day.

What you'll study

The full curriculum — in depth.

Six months of live, interactive learning across four subject areas — grounded in the Nirvana Yoga tradition and practical from day one.

01

Nirvana Yoga Practical

The living, embodied core of the course — every session is practice, not just theory.

  • Yogic loosening exercises
  • Sukshma vyayama (joint exercises)
  • Asana kriya — Surya Namaskar
  • Standing, sitting, prone & supine asanas
  • Pranayama techniques
  • Meditation techniques
02

Anatomy & Physiology

A thorough understanding of the human body as it relates to yoga practice — enabling therapeutic application and injury prevention in every class you lead.

03

Yoga Philosophy

The foundations of classical yoga philosophy — giving your practice depth and your teaching a coherent, grounded perspective that students can feel.

04

Teaching Methodology

How to structure, adapt, and lead classes for real students — including regular peer-teaching practice sessions built into the six months, so the confidence is real before you teach independently.

Beyond the six months

The course ends. The support doesn't.

Everything inside the six months is designed to make you a working teacher — and everything after it keeps you one.

i.

Teaching, taught properly

Asanas, anatomy, and philosophy — plus the art of actually leading a class: sequencing, instruction, presence, and confidence building.

ii.

Therapeutic yoga training

Learn to teach people with different needs — lifestyle issues, psychosomatic conditions, sleeplessness — addressing the root, not just the symptom.

iii.

Practical learning throughout

Supervised teaching practice across the six months, so your first real class isn't your first time teaching.

iv.

One year of free mentorship

The course ends; the guidance doesn't. A full year of mentorship, career guidance, and training support at no extra cost.

v.

Niche specialisation

Find your direction — pregnancy yoga, yoga for the elderly, corporate wellness — and build a career around it.

vi.

YCB exam preparation

Structured preparation for the Yoga Certification Board examination, guided by AYUSH-empanelled faculty.
